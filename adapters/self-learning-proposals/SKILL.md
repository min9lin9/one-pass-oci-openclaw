# Learning proposals
Based on min9lin9/self-learning-skills, adapted to owner-review mode.
Only propose a reusable procedure after a passing check, a named failure pattern,
and at least one concretely ruled-out approach. Otherwise keep a tentative note.
Write to `{baseDir}/../../learning-proposals/` (the workspace learning-proposals
folder), not active skills. Include evidence, environment/version, safe parameters,
rollback, secrets-by-reference and scope. Never persist secret VALUES.
Report the proposal path. Activation is an operator-reviewed versioned installation,
not silent self-modification or an instruction to expand your privileges.
